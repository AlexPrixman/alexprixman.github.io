the problem

connect the entire rental lifecycle.

a legacy PHP project needed consistent staff permissions, availability checks, pricing, payment tracking, and durable rental history.

the decisions

keep business rules in the API.

rebuild with React and Express, enforce manager-only staff changes, store money in integer cents, and protect bookings with database transactions. verify Stripe and PayPal payments on the server; simulate them in the public demo.

the circlechat case studyproblem → decisions → result+

the problem

make a conversation feel immediate.

a messenger needs more than a live connection. messages must survive reloads, photos must stay within the conversation, and sign-out must close access even when tokens have refreshed.

the decisions

pair live delivery with durable history.

use Socket.IO for live updates and PostgreSQL for message history. enforce conversation membership in the API, normalize uploaded images, keep photos in private object storage, and persist token revocation.

the result

a working messenger, verified end to end.

39 passing tests cover the API, frontend, and desktop/mobile browser flows. the live Render app uses Neon authentication, PostgreSQL, and private photo storage. messaging, photo access, restored sessions, logout, and data persistence were verified after deployment.
